Building Technology With Intention

I am a software engineer, researcher, and founder focused on building intelligent systems and products that are human-centered, community-driven, and impact-oriented.

Hi! My name is Giovanna.

I’m a software engineer, creative technologist, and community-driven builder passionate about using technology, storytelling, and design to create meaningful impact.

I currently work as a Software Engineer at Richemont, where I design and build digital solutions that support enterprise-scale operations and innovation. My work is deeply inspired by Meta’s mission to connect people and empower communities through technology. Alongside my full-time role, I take on freelance projects that allow me to blend engineering, design, and strategy for brands and individuals.

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics from Southern Connecticut State University. My academic journey includes hands-on research in Agentic AI and Large Language Models (LangChain, LLM agents, automation systems), as well as building projects like my AI Smart Mirror. I was also the first Computer Science Club President at Gateway Community College, where I helped create opportunities for students entering tech.

I am deeply passionate about women’s empowerment, community development, and equity. I am the founder of the Young Women’s Initiative (formerly Tech Goddesses) and a co-founder of the Puzzles Organization, where I focus on fundraising, advocacy, and inclusive programming.

I was selected as one of fewer than five students to attend the Regional Legislative Breakfast, where I built personal connections with state representatives, senators, and university leaders. I am also an alumna of the Women in Leadership Academy, which sparked my love for public speaking and civic engagement. My work centers on addressing systemic challenges faced by Latinos, women, and communities impacted by poverty. I also actively volunteer for environmental sustainability initiatives.

I am the founder of The Market Mind, where I explore the intersection of AI, marketing, and business strategy. Through freelancing and independent ventures, I build scalable systems, digital products, and creative solutions for modern brands.

I create content across digital platforms and am launching a newsletter soon, where I share my perspective on corporate life, technology, business, creativity, and ambition — navigating big dreams while staying grounded and intentional.

On a personal note, my dream is to travel to every country in the world, explore all U.S. states, islands, and regions of Brazil. I believe exposure to different cultures, lifestyles, and perspectives is essential to growth. Most of my inspiration comes from stepping outside my comfort zone. Fashion and self-expression are also central to who I am, and I love sharing my style and creative vision online.

With a strong hustler mindset, I believe balance is essential. I value mental, emotional, and physical well-being and strive to promote a lifestyle that prioritizes health, self-awareness, and sustainability alongside ambition.
